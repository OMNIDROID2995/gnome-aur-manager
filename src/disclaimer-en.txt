With gnome-aur-manager you can search, build, install and uninstall AUR packages. Installation is at your own risk – with well-known maintainers and widely used packages there is usually no danger.

Use the button below to install AppStream PackageKit integration, enabling official Arch repositories in GNOME Software Center. Native packages offer better speed and integration, Flatpak offers stronger isolation.

AUR apps are often built locally. Binary packages usually end in -bin. Stay at the terminal during installation to answer prompts and catch errors.
